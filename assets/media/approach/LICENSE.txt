Approach stage videos — sources and licences

stage-01.mp4 … stage-06.mp4 (with images/approach/stage-0N-video.webp and
stage-0N-video-end.webp, the first and final frames of each video)
  Rendered locally in Blender 5.2 LTS (EEVEE) from one master scene: the same
  studio (wall-mounted display, standing desk, engineer's workstation), lighting
  and camera system in all six stages. Credits are shown on the
  page through the "Credits" control in the corner of the animation.

  Characters (both rigs: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, CC BY 4.0,
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). None of the characters are
  CC0; the CC BY credits below are required.

  1. The designer (all stages) — adapted from the "Snow" character rig by Blender Studio
     Source: https://studio.blender.org/characters/snow/v4/
     Required credit: Snow Rig (CC) Blender Foundation | studio.blender.org
     Kept from Snow: skeleton and controls, skinning, facial rig and corrective
     shapes, eyes, ears, hands, the body and garment base meshes, and the painted
     skin detail.
     Changed for this portfolio:
       - Face proportions: narrower nose with a smaller tip, slightly lower bridge,
         thinner and narrower lips, slimmer jaw and chin, straighter and slimmer
         brows; a calmer default eyelid position in the animation.
       - Skin: skin textures recoloured to a lighter warm tone; subsurface reduced.
       - Hairstyle: Snow's top-knot hair replaced with a new layered short cut
         (strand groom with a side-parted fringe and soft, layered sides that reach
         the top of the ears).
       - Build: fuller shoulders, upper arms, forearms and chest.
       - Clothing: the scoop neckline reshaped into a crew neck with a new ribbed
         collar, fitted black T-shirt, dark indigo trousers, white sneakers.
       - New thin metal glasses.
       - Posed and animated for these scenes.

  2. The engineer (stage 05) — the "Snow" character rig by Blender Studio
     Source: https://studio.blender.org/characters/snow/v4/
     Required credit: Snow Rig (CC) Blender Foundation | studio.blender.org
     Changed for this portfolio: clothing recoloured (sage T-shirt, stone
     trousers); posed and animated for this scene, seated at a workstation. Face, hair and skin are
     Snow's own, so he is a different person from the designer.

  3. The support lead (stage 04) — adapted from the "Rain" character rig by Blender Studio
     Source: https://studio.blender.org/characters/rain/v3/
     Required credit: Rain Rig (CC) Blender Foundation | studio.blender.org
     Changed for this portfolio: smaller head on the same body (adult
     proportions), brown eyes, slate-blue top, rust neck scarf, charcoal
     trousers, dark hairband, ponytail posed to hang; posed and animated for
     this scene.
